Ramadan with Kids: 7 Gentle Traditions

Ramadan is the family's yearly spiritual classroom. Children won't remember every rule — they will remember how Ramadan felt. Here are seven gentle traditions to start this year.

  1. Make suhoor a cozy family moment — even little ones who aren't fasting can join for a few minutes.
  2. A good-deeds jar — one kind act a day, written on a slip and dropped in the jar.
  3. Hang a lantern and count down the days — a visible countdown builds excitement.
  4. Read one Prophet story each night — short, warm, and before bed.
  5. Let little ones help prepare iftar — setting dates on a plate counts!
  6. Make du'a together before breaking the fast — let them hear you mention them by name.
  7. Give each child a small sadaqah box — and decide together where it goes on Eid.

Pick two or three — not all seven. Small traditions done every year become the memories your children carry for life.
