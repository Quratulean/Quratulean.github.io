About

Tabasum Majeed is the creator of Islamic Parenting 101, a platform dedicated to sharing beneficial knowledge and practical guidance on raising children with strong iman, love for Allah, and good character.

She is a student and researcher with a deep interest in Islamic parenting and personal development, aiming to contribute toward a better future society through conscious, faith-centered upbringing.

Her work simplifies Islamic parenting into clear, actionable steps that help families build a peaceful and nurturing home — helping parents move from reactive parenting to an intentional, heart-centered approach: nurturing children's hearts before shaping their habits.

To help families raise children whose hearts are connected to Allah, whose character is grounded in adab, and whose homes are shaped by mercy, reflection, and intentional parenting.
