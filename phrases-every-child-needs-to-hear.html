10 Phrases Every Child Needs to Hear

The Prophet ﷺ taught, "If one of you loves his brother, let him tell him" (Abu Dawud and Tirmidhi). Children need to hear love, not just feel it. These ten phrases build a child's heart:

  1. "I love you — no matter what."
  2. "I'm so proud of how hard you tried."
  3. "You are such a kind person."
  4. "I trust you."
  5. "Allah made you perfectly."
  6. "You can come to me with anything."
  7. "I believe in you."
  8. "I notice how much effort you put in."
  9. "I love being your parent."
  10. "You can always come back to me. I will always be here."

Try this tonight: pick the phrase you say the least, and say it sincerely before bedtime. Watch their face.
