# Will "Hearts Before Habits" sell on Amazon? — Honest outlook (23 Nov 2026)

## Short answer
Yes, it can sell, but **Amazon will not sell it for you**. A brand-new self-published book with no reviews and no audience usually sells a handful of copies unless the author brings readers to the page. Your plan (Pinterest + blog + email list) is the right engine; it just takes months to build. Expect single digits to low double digits of sales in the first 3 months, growing if the list and reviews grow. These are my estimates, not guarantees.

## What the research showed
- **Most self-published books earn little.** In a 2025 survey cited by Inkfluence AI (a vendor, so read with care), 44% of 1,346 authors earned $100/month or less; about 80% of authors with 1–3 books earned under $100/month.
- **An email list is the biggest lever.** Same source: authors with a list had a median of ~$300/month vs ~$15 without. This is why the free gift + sign-up form matter.
- **Pinterest can work for books, but slowly.** Jane Friedman's guest author reports Pinterest became her top traffic source (about 84,000 monthly impressions, 5,000+ outbound clicks in a month), but she has 61 titles and years of pinning. Pins that sent people to a free lead magnet built her email list (one coached author got 1,200+ subscribers in six months).
- **The niche is small but not crowded.** On Goodreads, established Islamic parenting titles have anywhere from a few to a few hundred ratings each (e.g. *Positive Parenting in the Muslim Home*: 101 ratings). That means demand exists, but nobody dominates; a warm, practical, mom-focused book has room. Amazon's own search pages could not be read, so I could not check Amazon rankings or prices directly.

## Money per copy (KDP, Amazon.com)
Royalty = 60% of list price minus printing cost (for books priced $9.99+). Printing cost (rates from a third-party KDP calculator; please confirm in KDP's own calculator): premium color $1.00 + $0.065/page; standard color $1.00 + $0.0255/page. For your 74-page book:

| Interior | Print cost | Price $14.99 | Price $17.99 | Price $19.99 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Premium color | $5.81 | $3.18 | $4.98 | $6.18 |
| Standard color | $2.89 | $6.10 | $7.90 | $9.10 |

Premium color at $14.99 leaves about half of what standard color does. Order a proof of standard color and compare quality before choosing.

## What sales to expect (my assumptions, not data)
Funnel: pin → blog visit → click to Amazon → buy. Assumptions: 3–6% of blog visitors click through to the book page; 3–6% of those buy (a new book with no reviews converts at the low end).
| Scenario | Blog visits / month | Book sales / month |
|---|---|---|
| Slow start (Jan–Feb) | ~300 | under 1 |
| Steady (pinning 5/week for 3–4 months) | ~1,500 | ~2–5 |
| Strong (Ramadan + email list of 500) | ~5,000 + list | ~15–20 + launch-week list sales |

So the earlier goal of 25 sales by the end of Ramadan is only realistic with a launch to an email list and friends/family/community. A fair target: **10–25 copies by end of Ramadan 2027** if the list reaches 200–500.

## What to do to improve the odds
1. **Collect emails now** (free Calm Mama Starter Kit) and launch the book to that list.
2. **Get 10–20 honest reviews at launch** by giving advance copies to moms in your community and asking for an honest review (never pay or trade for reviews; Amazon forbids it).
3. **Price and format:** consider standard color; $14.99–$17.99; add a Kindle edition (no printing cost) and a direct PDF edition (e.g. Gumroad/Payhip) at a lower price.
4. **Optimise the Amazon page:** subtitle with the words moms search ("A Gentle Islamic Parenting Guide for Muslim Mothers"), two good categories, 7 keyword phrases, "Look inside", author page.
5. **Link every pin/article to the book page only after it is live**, and add the Amazon link to book.html and the blog CTA.
6. **Consider small Amazon Ads (a few dollars a day) only after reviews arrive.** Results are uncertain.
7. **Keep the free value coming** (articles + printables). Trust is what turns readers into buyers.
